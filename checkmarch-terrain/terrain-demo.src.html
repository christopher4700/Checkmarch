  • Walls. Rock and water stop bishops, rooks and queens before them. Nothing can land on them, so nothing can be captured there.
  • Knights leap. A knight's jump passes over terrain; only a blocked landing square is lost.
  • Pawns cannot push into terrain; a double step needs both squares open.
  • Fair ground. No terrain below tier 3, then 2 to 6 squares (at most 3 at a citadel or keep), never on either side's two home ranks, never all four centre squares, and every open square stays reachable.
